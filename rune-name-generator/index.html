Maps modern letters to the closest available Elder Futhark signs. Common pairs such as th and ng can use one historical character.

02

Sound-aware approximation

Applies a limited set of common Polish, English or Norwegian pronunciation rules before selecting signs. It remains an approximation.

03

Elder, not Younger

These 24 signs belong to Elder Futhark, an earlier Germanic rune row. Viking Age Scandinavians usually used the 16-rune Younger Futhark.
